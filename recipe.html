Restaurant-Style Paneer Butter Masala

A classic North Indian curry made with soft paneer cubes simmered in a creamy, buttery tomato-cashew gravy.

Recipe Overview

  • Cuisine: North Indian (Punjabi)
  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 25 minutes
  • Total Time: 40 minutes
  • Servings: 4 servings

Ingredients

For the Tomato-Cashew Paste:

  • 4 large ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 10–12 whole raw cashews (soaked in warm water for 15 mins)
  • 1 inch ginger, peeled
  • 4–5 garlic cloves
  • 2 green cardamoms
  • 1 small cinnamon stick

For the Curry Base:

  • 250g fresh paneer (cottage cheese), cut into cubes
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 bay leaf (tej patta)
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (for color)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon crushed Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh heavy cream
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves, finely chopped (for garnish)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the gravy puree: Blend the chopped tomatoes, soaked cashews, ginger, garlic, cardamoms, and cinnamon stick together into a smooth paste.
  2. Temper the aromatics: Heat 1 tablespoon of butter and 1 tablespoon of oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the bay leaf and sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Cook the masala: Pour in the blended tomato-cashew puree. Add Kashmiri red chili powder, turmeric, and salt. Cook on medium-low flame for 10–12 minutes until the oil separates from the gravy sides.
  4. Simmer: Add 1/2 cup of warm water to adjust the gravy consistency. Bring to a gentle boil.
  5. Add paneer: Gently slide in the paneer cubes. Simmer on low heat for 3–4 minutes so the paneer absorbs the flavors (do not overcook, or paneer turns rubbery).
  6. Finish and enrich: Sprinkle garam masala and crushed Kasuri methi. Stir in the remaining 1 tablespoon of butter and heavy cream. Turn off the heat.
  7. Garnish & serve: Top with fresh coriander leaves and a swirl of cream. Serve hot.

Chef's Tips

  • Soft Paneer: If using store-bought paneer, soak the cubed paneer in warm water for 10 minutes before adding it to the curry.
  • Smooth Texture: For a true restaurant-style velvety gravy, strain the blended tomato-cashew puree through a fine-mesh sieve before cooking.

Serving suggestion: Best paired with hot garlic naan, tandoori roti, or jeera rice.
